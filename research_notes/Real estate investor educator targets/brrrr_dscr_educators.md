## Who are well-known buy-and-hold / BRRRR / rental portfolio investing coaches and educators running paid courses, coaching, or masterminds (verify named examples; find others not primarily tied to BiggerPockets staff roles)?

### Takeaway
A credible "Michael Chang tier" roster does exist beyond BiggerPockets: independent educators such as Chad "Coach" Carson, Craig Curelop/The FI Team, Michael Zuber, Eric Hughes/Rental Income Advisors, Ryan Chaw, Sam Primm/Faster Freedom, Cody Davis & Christian Osgood/Multifamily Strategy, Anna Kelley/REI Mom, Whitney Elkins-Hutten, Marco Santarelli/Norada, and the InvestHER community (Andresa Guidelli & Liz Faircloth) all run paid coaching, courses, or masterminds teaching buy-and-hold, BRRRR, house-hacking-to-portfolio, or turnkey rental strategies. Of the names suggested for verification, Coach Carson, Craig Curelop, and Ashley Kehr check out as real, active educators; David Greene and Dave Meyer remain heavily tied to BiggerPockets' core media (co-host roles) even though Greene has independent ventures, so they are flagged rather than treated as clean "non-BP" picks. - Several credible educators (David Greene, Whitney Elkins-Hutten, Ashley Kehr) sit in a "hybrid" zone — independently branded but still tied to BiggerPockets or a syndication platform — so outreach lists should tag these clearly rather than treating them as fully independent small creators.

- Whitney Elkins-Hutten: monetizes partly through her institutional role at PassiveInvesting.com (syndication placement) alongside direct coaching/education via AshWealth — a hybrid coaching + capital-raising monetization model — [source: search snippet]

- General pattern: none of the profiled sites disclosed vendor/lender/property-manager affiliate deals explicitly in available search snippets, though turnkey-model educators (Norada, Morris Invest) are functionally monetizing via property/vendor sales rather than pure affiliate commissions.

### Inferences
- Because most of these businesses are lead-gen funnels (free webinar/training → paid coaching or mastermind), a mortgage lender could plausibly pursue either (a) a content/webinar co-sponsorship arrangement or (b) a preferred-lender referral/affiliate arrangement with the coaching-model educators (Curelop, Hughes, Chaw, Primm, Davis/Osgood), since these businesses already routinely refer clients to lenders, property managers, and other vendors as part of their coaching process.
- The turnkey-property-sales model (Norada, and especially Morris Invest) monetizes differently — through property markups/commissions rather than course fees — which is a materially different partnership structure (and higher risk, per the Morris Invest lawsuits) than the coaching/course educators.

### Gaps
- Exact price points for every paid course/coaching/mastermind program were not disclosed in public search snippets; each would require a direct site visit or a "book a call" step to obtain current pricing (found true for House Hacking University, Rental Income Advisors, Newbie Real Estate Investing, Faster Freedom, and Multifamily Strategy). This is consistent with how creator-led coaching businesses typically gate pricing behind a sales call, but it means none of the price points above could be cited with a number.
- No explicit lender/property-manager affiliate-deal disclosures were found for any name on this list; absence of evidence is not evidence of absence — these relationships often aren't publicly disclosed and would need to be confirmed via direct outreach or an affiliate-network lookup.
